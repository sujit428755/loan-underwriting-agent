# Retail Unsecured Personal Loan Policy (synthetic, for demonstration)

## 1. Eligibility

### P-1.1 Age
Applicant age must be between 21 and 60 years at application. Age plus loan tenure must not exceed 65 years at maturity. Applications outside this range are declined.

### P-1.2 Employment stability
Salaried applicants need at least 12 months with the current employer. Applicants with 6 to 12 months are referred for manual review. Applicants with less than 6 months are declined.

## 2. Income and exposure

### P-2.1 Minimum income
Verified net monthly income must be at least INR 25,000. Verified income is the lower of the salary slip net pay and the average salary credit in the bank statement.

### P-2.2 Loan to income
Requested loan amount must not exceed 12 times verified net monthly income. Between 12 and 15 times the case is referred for manual review. Above 15 times it is declined.

## 3. Affordability

### P-3.1 Debt to income
Post-loan debt to income (existing EMIs plus the new EMI, divided by verified net monthly income) must not exceed 40 percent. Between 40 and 50 percent the case is referred for manual review. Above 50 percent it is declined.

## 4. Credit conduct

### P-4.1 Credit score
Bureau credit score of 700 or above passes. Scores from 650 to 699 are referred for manual review. Scores below 650 are declined.

### P-4.2 Repayment conduct
No more than one cheque or auto-debit bounce in the last 6 months of bank statements. Two bounces are referred for manual review. Three or more are declined.

## 5. Verification and fraud

### P-5.1 Income verification
The salary slip net pay must be within 10 percent of the average monthly salary credit in the bank statement. A larger gap is treated as a possible document manipulation and referred to fraud review before any other decision.

### P-5.2 Identity consistency
Applicant name and employer must be consistent across the application form, salary slip, bank statement and credit report. Any mismatch is referred to fraud review.

## 6. Documentation

### P-6.1 Required documents
A complete file contains the application form, salary slip, six months of bank statements and a credit bureau report. The system must not issue a credit recommendation on an incomplete file. It requests the missing documents instead.

## 7. Model risk and governance

### P-7.1 Probability of default
Model probability of default below 10 percent passes. From 10 to 25 percent the case is referred for manual review. Above 25 percent it is declined.

### P-7.2 Human decision authority
The automated system only recommends. Every final decision requires approval from a human underwriter. An underwriter who overrides the recommendation must record a reason.

### P-7.3 Fair lending
Gender, religion, caste, marital status and similar protected attributes must not be used as model inputs or decision rules. Recommendation rates are monitored across groups using the four-fifths rule.
